STUDENT'S EVALUATION OF FACULTY TO THE STUDENT: This faculty rating scale is designed to help improve instruction for your benefit as well as the faculty's. It contains questions which you are to answer carefully and objectively. Your answers will be kept in strict confidence, and your identity will not be revealed.

You may do the evaluation starting today by clicking the START EVALUATION button and please do this for all your classes. If you miss to evaluate a class, you will not be able to view your grade in that class.

PROCEDURE / INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Click START EVALUATION button.
2. Choose the letter (a, b, c, d or e) which corresponds to the answer in the questionnaire.
3. With the particular faculty in mind, carefully consider which answer most nearly indicated your answer to the question being asked then you choose the letter which corresponds to your final answer.
4. If you have any further comments about this faculty, write them briefly at the bottom of the questionnaire.
5. Click SUBMIT button if finished.
6. Once submitted, you will be able to view your grade in that class.
